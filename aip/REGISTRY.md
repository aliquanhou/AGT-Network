## AIP Statuses

- **Draft**: Proposal is being written
- **Discussion**: Open for community feedback (minimum 14 days)
- **Review**: Under formal review by maintainers
- **Accepted**: Approved, pending or in implementation
- **Rejected**: Not accepted (with rationale)
- **Withdrawn**: Withdrawn by author
- **Superseded**: Replaced by a later AIP

## Proposing an AIP

1. Read [AIP-0](AIP-0.md) for the process
2. Copy the template from AIP-0
3. Open an issue or PR with your proposal
4. The registry will be updated when your AIP is accepted

## AIP Numbering

AIP numbers are assigned sequentially and never reused. To propose a new AIP, take the next available number.
